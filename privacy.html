Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how "Hermes Agent — Personal Automation" (the "app") handles information when used with Google services and Google APIs.

1. Who uses this app

This app is a personal automation tool operated solely by its owner for the owner's own Google account. It is not distributed to, or used by, any other users.

2. What data the app accesses

With the owner's consent, the app may access the following Google account data via Google APIs:

  • Gmail — messages, labels, and drafts (read, modify, send)
  • Google Calendar — events and calendars (read, create, edit)
  • Google Drive — files and folders (read, upload, organize)
  • Google Docs and Google Sheets — documents and spreadsheets (read, create, edit)
  • Google Contacts — contact list (read only)

3. How data is used

All data accessed by the app is used exclusively to perform automation tasks requested by the owner (e.g., scheduled backups, mail triage, calendar briefings). Data is processed locally on the owner's computer. The app does not:

  • sell, rent, or trade any data;
  • share data with any third party;
  • transfer data to any external server other than Google's own APIs;
  • use data for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

4. Data storage and security

OAuth tokens and any locally cached data remain on the owner's machine. Access credentials are stored with standard operating-system protections and are never published. The owner may revoke the app's access at any time via their Google Account permissions page.

5. Contact

For any questions about this policy or the app, contact the project owner via the user support email listed on the OAuth consent screen.
