Hermes Agent — Personal Automation

Hermes Agent is a personal automation assistant used by a single user (the project owner). It connects to Google Workspace services — Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Google Contacts — to perform routine tasks on the owner's behalf, such as:

  • Reading and organizing email
  • Reading calendar events and creating reminders
  • Backing up and synchronizing files to Google Drive
  • Reading and editing documents and spreadsheets

The app requests access only to the owner's own Google account. No data is collected, shared, or sold to any third party. All processing happens locally on the owner's machine.
